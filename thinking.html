Thinking

Ideas I keep coming back to.

The finance lens is useful far beyond markets. These are the mental models I use when evaluating an opportunity, a project or my own next move.

01

Price is not value.

A visible number can be precise while the underlying assumptions remain uncertain. Always ask what expectations are embedded in the price.

VALUATION
02

Process beats prediction.

The strongest advantage is often a repeatable decision process that remains useful when the outcome is uncertain.

DECISION-MAKING
03

Communication is leverage.

If the analysis cannot survive a two-minute explanation, the thinking probably needs another pass.

COMMUNICATION
04

Discipline compounds.

Small improvements made consistently create a stronger base than occasional bursts of intensity.

EXECUTION
A simple loop

Observe → Frame → Test → Review

Use the loop for markets, projects, research and personal development. The point is to keep learning faster than the environment changes.

01Observe what is actually happening
02Frame the problem clearly
03Test the strongest explanation
04Review what changed your mind
